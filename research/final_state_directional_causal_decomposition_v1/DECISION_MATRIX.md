# Final-State Directional Causal Decomposition v1: decision matrix

Status: prospective and subordinate to the integrity and effect-reconstruction
gates in [PREREGISTRATION.md](PREREGISTRATION.md).

Let `epsilon = 0.04405641704135471`. “Equivalent” means the stratified
bootstrap 90% interval is wholly inside `[-epsilon,+epsilon]`. A substantial
inherited-direction contribution means its 95% interval is wholly below
`-epsilon`, because the inherited `G_full` direction is negative.
An opposing substantial contribution means its 95% interval is wholly above
`+epsilon`. “Directional but under-resolved” means a non-equivalent residual
whose 95% interval is wholly below zero but not wholly below `-epsilon`. All
remaining non-equivalent cases are inconclusive. These definitions make the
rows below mutually exclusive after applying them from top to bottom.

| Outcome | Hypothesis consequence | Decision | Next study |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audit or numerical identity fails | No FSD hypothesis tested | **NO-GO** for interpretation | Repair/rerun v1 if contract unchanged; otherwise v2 |
| `G_full` fails the frozen reconstruction gate | FSD1 weakened; there is no stable inherited effect to decompose | **NO-GO** for FSD2/FSD3 | Measurement/Reproduction Audit v1; no localization or rescue |
| `G_R` equivalent to zero; `G_T` reconstructs `G_full` | Supports FSD2; weakens FSD3; teacher axis is sufficient for the direct final-state margin effect | **GO** | Upstream Teacher-Path Localization v1; rescue is unnecessary at this bottleneck and should be used only upstream |

| Any other non-equivalent result | Mechanistic outcome unresolved | **NO-GO** for rescue/localization claims | Increase prompts prospectively; do not add directions or controls |

## Interpretation constraints

Teacher-axis sufficiency at the final bottleneck does not prove unique upstream
mediation. Residual dominance does not identify a residual feature. Mixed or
cancelling outcomes do not authorize post-hoc direction fitting inside this
study. Every downstream study must preserve the component definitions or
declare a new versioned contract.

Prompt-family estimates are descriptive modifiers only. Opposing family point
estimates qualify the evidence boundary but do not overturn or replace the
pooled primary equivalence decision.
