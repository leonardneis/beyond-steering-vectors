# C18 v2: prospective disjoint decision matrix

Status: frozen before C18-v2 scientific execution. Apply rows top to bottom.

Definitions:

- `Eq90(B)`: marginal 90% interval strictly inside `(-epsilon,+epsilon)`.
- `Mat99(X)`: marginal 99% interval wholly below `-epsilon` or above
  `+epsilon`.
- `Cancel`: any preregistered 25x72 cancellation rule fires.
- `SuffixConflict`: material W interaction `J`, material Z/W background
  dependence, or opposing material U/S allocation.

| Order | Class | Exact condition | Maximum claim | Forbidden interpretation |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **F -- TECHNICAL / IDENTIFICATION FAILURE** | any hard technical, inventory, natural-effect, Z/W reconstruction, or algebra gate fails | no C18 hypothesis tested | any interpretation of partial outputs |
| 2 | **B -- ASYMMETRIC TRANSPORT** | exactly one of `Eq90(B0)`, `Eq90(B1)` holds and the other residual is `Mat99` | one interchange direction is practically adequate and the other materially is not; background-conditioned interchange | bidirectional transport; autonomous necessity or sufficiency |
| 3 | **C -- STATE x PARAMETER INTERACTION / CO-ADAPTATION** | no earlier row; `Mat99(I)` or `SuffixConflict` | the controlled coordinate effect depends materially on parameter background or cut-state-by-suffix | semantic synergy, natural interaction effect, or upstream localization |
| 4 | **E -- CANCELLATION / HETEROGENEITY LIMITED** | both B residuals are `Eq90`, `Cancel`, and no C criterion holds | only pooled G residuals are practically small | global transport, prompt generality, or selecting a favorable subgroup |
| 5 | **A -- BIDIRECTIONAL FUNCTIONAL TRANSPORT** | both B residuals are `Eq90`; no `Cancel`; no `SuffixConflict`; all Z/W gates valid | the frozen coordinate family is functionally interchangeable/adequate for the fixed G contrast on both backgrounds | natural/unique mediation, whole-trait explanation, universal circuit |
| 6 | **D -- TEACHER-COORDINATE INSUFFICIENCY** | no earlier row; both residuals are not equivalent and at least one is `Mat99` | the tested coordinates do not adequately remove the fixed parameter residual | teacher information is irrelevant; residual is one feature or high-dimensional |
| 7 | **G -- INSUFFICIENT PRECISION / UNRESOLVED** | every other technically valid outcome | interchange remains undecided | no-effect claim, post-hoc direction/layer rescue |

Class B is reported separately although asymmetry implies background
dependence; Class C is reserved for directly identified interaction or suffix
conflict outside the preceding asymmetric phenotype. Orthogonal-dose
specificity is one secondary modifier and never changes A--G classification.
