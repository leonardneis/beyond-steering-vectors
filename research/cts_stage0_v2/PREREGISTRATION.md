## 1. Purpose and logical role

Stage 0 is an instrument-adequacy and label check in the base model. It asks: does the teacher persona
geometry contain a reliable minimal-pair contrast direction that, added at the Stage-2 site, selectively raises the
target trait's answer probability beyond template-matched and random nulls, robustly to dose and wording, **and
whose selectivity is not carried by the constructed shared persona components**? It also asks whether the
constructed shared directions behave as shared (non-selective) directions.

**Operator link to Stage 2.** Stage 2a interchanges the c-coordinate between a subliminal student S and a neutral
student N at slot 14, last prompt token. S→N interchange sets h_N' = h_N + ⟨h_S − h_N, c⟩ c, an additive shift of
the N state along c. Last-token additive steering in the base is therefore the base analogue of the Stage-2
operator. What does not transfer: the dose (student scale is unknown and must stay unknown here), distributed
weight changes (Stage-2 ceiling gate), and whether students move along c at all.

A pass licenses only labels about base-model directions. A fail licenses only statements about this estimator,
site and dose. Neither licenses any claim about students.

Prior predictions (non-binding, stated before any v2 forward): TS(a–e) pass for c_cat,dog ≈ 0.7 (unchanged from
v1); TS(f) pass given TS(a–e) ≈ 0.6; mention control not excluded ≈ 0.6; BASE_SHARED_DIRECTION for g_anim given a
passing contrast ≈ 0.4.

## 8. Criteria

α_TS = 0.05/3 (k = 3). Paired prompt bootstrap as v1 (spec `statistics.bootstrap`). Rank tests:
p = (1 + #{T_null ≥ T_obs})/(1 + n), pass iff p < α.

- **R:** split-half cosine ≥ 0.95 at slot 14.
- **TS(c)** for c ∈ {c_cat,dog, c_cat,wolf, c_cat,anim}, all of:
  - (a) R(c).
  - (b) direction and reversibility (v1 §8.2b), including Δℓ_{X,O}(−c) > 0 for c_cat,X.
  - (c) selectivity: every pairwise CI_low > 0 over O (and X-selectivity under −c for c_cat,X).
  - (d1) structured-null rank test on T = Δℓ_{cat,O}(+c): at most 3 of 240 nulls ≥ T.
  - (d2) R_cov rank test on T at magnitude |τ(c)|: at most 2 of 199 ≥ T.
  - (e) point-estimate signs of (b) at κ = 0.5 and with the paraphrase contrasts (T2, T3; T3 only for c_cat,anim,
    described as robustness to one paraphrase only).
  - **(f) new:** R(c⊥G) ≥ 0.95, and (b) and (c) hold for c⊥G at its own teacher coordinate.
  - For c_cat,anim, O is replaced by O' = {fox, owl, turtle, spider, ant} throughout.
- **PC:** CI_low Δℓ_{cat,O}(t_cat) > 0 and rank test vs the first 99 R_cov at ‖t_cat‖ (at most 3 of 99 ≥ T,
  α_PC = 0.05). **PC-pos:** same at all positions. **PC*:** unit(t_cat) at |τ(c)|, CI_low > 0.
- **A1–A3** decided in v1 (by hash); **A4** window [0.01, 0.5] (heuristic, unchanged); **A5** = TS(c_cat,X);
  **A6** persona P_X vs `persona:P_default`, CI_low Δℓ_{X,O} > 0; **A7** raw t_X, CI_low > 0.

The structured-null rank test is a calibrated benchmark: exchangeability of c with the null contrasts is a
modelling assumption, because c contains cat content by construction. Its stringency is set by the 16 frozen
words, which are not subsampled.

## 9. Integrity, including numerical fragility

### 9.1 Integrity checks (TECHNICAL_FAIL on any failure)

Spec `integrity_checks`: input hashes; render identity; last-three-token assertion; hook tests (change at every slot
> site); no NaN/inf; completeness; baseline repeat on a second host within 1e-4 per form; sidecars carry commit and
identity digest; no D/C id; runtime re-tokenization; fragility (§9.2); accounting ledger within cap.

### 9.2 Decision-level fragility (new)

- **What is re-scored:** the L2 baseline, all 47 gating named L2 conditions, and the first 10 members of each of
  the 7 L2 null families (70), in the reference layout L1 (v1 implementation), on their prompt sets: 118
  conditions, 37,032 prompt-conditions. Own-prefix conditions are identical in L2 and L1.
- **Standardized shifts:** z_S = (S_L2 − S_L1)/SE_boot(S_L2) for every gating or label statistic S computable from
  re-scored conditions (SE_boot = SD of the 10,000 paired bootstrap replicates); z_j = (T_j,L2 − T_j,L1)/SD_F for
  every re-scored null condition j of family F (SD_F = SD of the L2 statistics over all members of F). All m shifts
  are pooled; m is counted and reported (≈ 320 by the draft's enumeration).
- **Criterion:** PASS iff (1) RMS(z) ≤ ε **and** (2) max |z| ≤ ε·q_m, q_m = Φ⁻¹(1 − 0.05/(2m)) (≈ 3.78 for m = 321,
  i.e. max |z| ≤ 0.151).
- **ε = 0.04.** Derivation in §13.1.
- **Consequence:** any violation is TECHNICAL_FAIL (FRAGILITY), not rerunnable. It is evaluated automatically
  before any decision criterion.

### 9.3 Why the gate is in-run and not in TV-v2

On V (40 prompts), the layout bias of a prompt-mean cannot be estimated precisely enough. For a statistic with
per-prompt SD s_x and per-prompt layout difference SD s_d, the S0-level shift in SE units has a noise floor of
s_d/s_x; the bias term estimated from 40 prompts carries noise of order √(300/40)·s_d/s_x ≈ 2.7·s_d/s_x. With
plausible s_d/s_x ≈ 0.02 this noise alone exceeds ε. A TV gate would then fail or pass by chance. The in-run check
measures the actual statistics on the actual prompts and costs ≈ 2.2 warm A100-h. TV-v2 still reports a
descriptive V-level estimate.

## 13. Derivation of the three open values

### 13.1 ε = 0.04 (fragility)

- The relevant scale of a layout shift is the uncertainty of the statistic it could move, hence ε·SE.
- The frozen design already accepts random Monte-Carlo imprecision in every CI bound: with n_boot = 10,000, the MC
  standard error of the percentile bound at p = 1 − α_TS/2 = 0.99167 is, under a normal approximation,
  √(p(1−p)/B)/φ(z_p) = 0.000909/0.0227 = **0.040 SE**.
- A deterministic layout shift of at most this size cannot move a decision more often than the bootstrap's own MC
  noise already does. The worst-case probability that a shift of 0.04 SE flips a CI decision is ≤ 0.04·φ(0) ≈ 1.6 %,
  attained only when the true effect sits exactly at the boundary.
- Candidates rejected: 0.1 (a round convention without anchor); 0.25 (would allow shifts of a quarter SE, larger than
  any other accepted imprecision in the design).
- **Multiplicity.** The anchor is a per-statistic scale, and the MC noise it describes is itself random across
  statistics. The criterion therefore compares like with like: the RMS of the ≈ 320 standardized layout shifts must
  not exceed 0.04 (the RMS of the accepted MC noise). A per-statistic maximum at ε would fail with near certainty
  even in the accepted regime (draft audit, finding 1). The max guard ε·q_m catches a single gross shift; q_m is set
  so that shifts distributed as N(0, ε²) exceed it with probability ≤ 0.05 across all m statistics. No new constant
  is introduced: 0.05 is the design's family-wise level.
- Whether ε = 0.04 is attainable is an empirical question. The v1 TV numbers (word-level per-prompt p99 0.017) make
  it plausible but do not establish it. If the check fails, the result would be rounding-fragile at the design's
  precision, and v2 stops with TECHNICAL_FAIL. This is the intended behaviour.

### 13.2 r = 0.20 (BASE_SHARED_DIRECTION margin)

- **No absolute margin in nats is justified by existing evidence.** Any fixed nat value would be arbitrary without
  outcome data, and data from earlier project studies use other prompts and renderings.
- The label's purpose is to let Stage 2 read "g carries both traits" as shared transfer. That reading fails if g
  itself carries cat-selective information comparable to what Stage 2 would call "carrying". Stage 2's frozen
  smallest effect of interest for "carries" is 0.20 of the ceiling (v1 §12). The same fraction, applied at equal
  norm to the trait-specific contrast, gives the margin: g is "not trait-selective" if its selectivity is below a
  fifth of c's for every relevant pair.
- The relative margin is implemented as a two-sided equivalence test on linear combinations under the paired
  bootstrap (the synthesis form used for relative non-inferiority margins), so the margin's own sampling
  uncertainty is included; no ratio of noisy quantities is formed.
- Classification: heuristic smallest effect of interest, inherited. It is a label; it never gates.

### 13.3 Accounting rule

- **Count every scientific attempt** (researcher decision): makes the cap a hard property and removes "infrastructure"
  as a classification degree of freedom.
- **Reserve 0.8 × cap:** v1 convention, retained. No failure-rate data exist to derive a reserve; with shards
  ≤ 0.5 × retirement (E3), the 6 A100-h reserve covers ≈ 12 lost shards.
- **TV cap 6 A100-h, 3 attempts:** TV-v2 is estimated at 2–3 A100-h; the cap allows about two repeats after
  engineering fixes. TV is outcome-blind, so extending it is a resource decision, not a forking risk.
- **ENG uncapped but barred from S0/D/C:** development cannot touch scientific prompts, so its cost carries no
  inferential risk.
